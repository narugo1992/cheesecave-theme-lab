What the site makes of it (contrast, WCAG)

Three methods, same colour

Each method gets the same brand colour. Rules is the hand-written OKLCH logic from this project; Material 3 is Google's tonal-palette algorithm; Ant Design is the generator of that design system. Page and card style applies to Rules only: the other two bring their own greys.

Only changing the hue

The same lightness and vividness as the colour above, twelve hues, through the selected method. Click one to use it. Yellow, green and cyan cannot be as vivid as blue or purple at the same lightness; where the colour turns dull, that is the colour space, not a bug.

How close does it get to a real theme?

Take a real theme, give the method only its light primary, and compare what comes back with the theme's own five other colours. A distance below 0.02 is barely visible, above 0.05 is clearly different.
