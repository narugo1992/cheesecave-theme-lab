Readability checks
What is decided for you, and why
  • Your colour is the light primary, unchanged.
  • Dark primary: the same hue and vividness, made lighter only when it is too dark for a dark page (lightness floor 0.68). In 13 real themes the dark accent is at the median only 0.12 lighter than the light one, and four use the same colour in both modes.
  • Page and card: near white in light mode, near black in dark mode, with a faint tint of your hue. The five tint styles the earlier prototype offered differed by at most 0.025 (barely visible), so they are no longer a choice. The dark page sits at lightness 0.23, the median of the 13 real themes (their range is 0.15 to 0.33).
  • One method: hand-written rules in OKLCH, which came closest to the real themes (mean distance 0.041 against 0.067 for Material 3 and 0.064 for Ant Design). The comparison is on the research page.
